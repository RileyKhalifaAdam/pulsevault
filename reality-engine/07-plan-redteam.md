# Reality engine — red-team review of the workshop-scale plan

Two adversarial reviews of `03-plan.md` as first written (before the solar-system and multiplayer requirements), on its own terms and as the future innermost region of a larger engine.

## Lens: physics

**Verdict.** The synthesized plan does fix the solver-level fatal flaws of the candidates (pairwise velocity-level impulses plus reduced-coordinate articulations at one global 480 steps per second; softened FEM and the single energy-minimising solver removed; probe first; mechanisms first). But it is a summary that keeps the names of the honesty machinery while dropping about 55 concrete review fixes, and three fatal-class physics defects survive in weaker form: break/yield loads that the rigid tier does not determine, explicit softening of granular and soft matter, and an unspecified lagged CPU-GPU coupling. Its charter is also internally inconsistent (uniform gravity is unlisted hard-coded physics; 'one sanctioned departure' is at least three; the governor changes outcomes; switching was 'dropped' yet melting and breaking need it). Against the new requirement the decisive problem is that Layer 1, declared permanent, describes a closed, flat, airless, single-frame, constant-g, one-clock world: it has no reference frames, no field laws, no open ledgered boundary and no hand-off contract, so it cannot be embedded in a larger engine without changing the permanent layer. No code exists, so all of this is cheap to correct now; none of it requires abandoning the laws-and-materials-over-replaceable-solvers idea. All numbers below are tagged; I re-derived them in a scratch calculation and fetched Box2D defaults and three constants from source, but did not re-verify the research's device figures.

### Issues
- **P01** [fatal, scale] Layer 1, declared permanent, has no reference-frame concept: state is implicitly absolute position and velocity from one origin in one inertial frame, at a precision the plan never states. That cannot be embedded on a rotating planet, in orbit, or anywhere far from the origin, and every solver kernel will be written against it. — Evidence: DERIVED: the per-substep gravity increment is g*dt^2 = 9.81/480^2 = 42.6 um, while f32 spacing 2^(e-23) is 61 um from 512 m, 0.5 m at Earth's radius (MEASURED 6371 km, Wikipedia 'Earth radius') and 16.4 km at 1 AU (MEASURED 149,597,870,700 m, Wikipedia); even f64 at 1 AU is 2^(37-52) = 30.5 um, above the 17 um static contact sag the STRATA physics review derived. DERIVED: low-orbit speed sqrt(GM/r) = sqrt(3.986e14/6.771e6) = 7.67 km/s is 16 m per substep. MEASURED: the Box2D lineage rescales any body faster than 400 m/s by default (box2d src/types.c maximumLinearSpeed and src/solver.c, fetched 2026-11-24). — Fix: Make the frame a first-class Layer 1 object before Phase 0b: every simulated region owns a frame (f64 or fixed-point origin, velocity, angular velocity, parent) and all solver state is frame-local deltas, with integer tick time. Add translation, rotation and Galilean-boost invariance gates plus a far-from-origin resting test to the Phase 0b suite.
- **P02** [fatal, scale] The plan models a closed world: it deleted representation switching, forbids creating matter or setting velocities, and describes everything outside the awake set only as 'sleeping or simplified matter'. The innermost region of a larger engine must continuously import and export bodies and matter across its boundary, which is exactly the creation, teleport and hand-off the plan has no conserving contract for. — Evidence: Plan lines 45-46, 72 and 110. The STRATA physics review's fix for 'bodies leaving the awake bubble have no stated representation, so their momentum can simply vanish' and for the particle-window flux boundary was not carried over. Without it the ladder's 'exact by construction: mass, momentum' holds only while nothing crosses the region edge. — Fix: Add to Layer 1 a boundary port with per-step flux accounts for mass, linear momentum, angular momentum and total energy, and a hand-off contract: aggregate mass, centre of mass, inertia tensor, momenta and energy must match across a representation change to a stated tolerance, with the residual booked. Reserve worst-case promotion cost at admission, and treat engine-internal hand-offs as an audited class separate from game escape hatches.
- **P03** [major, foundations] Charter rule 1 claims conservation laws are the only hard-coded physics, yet uniform constant gravity is a hard-coded force law the charter never names, and the ledger's potential energy is implicitly m*g*h in one frame. There is no place for a gravitational field, fictitious-force work, frame-transfer terms or radiative flux. — Evidence: DERIVED: g scales as (R/(R+h))^2, so -3.1% at 100 km and -11.5% at 400 km, and its direction tilts L/R = 1.6e-4 rad per km; Earth-surface Coriolis 2*7.292e-5*v is 1.5e-3 m/s^2 at 10 m/s, but in a 100 m radius habitat spun to 1 g (omega = sqrt(9.81/100) = 0.313 rad/s) it is 0.63 m/s^2 per m/s, 6.4% of g. DERIVED: Earth's orbital kinetic energy 0.5*5.97e24*29780^2 = 2.6e33 J (mass and speed from memory) has f64 spacing 2^59 = 5.8e17 J, so one global ledger cannot hold a planet and a robot. — Fix: Add a 'fields and interactions' class to the charter: Newtonian gravitation is the law, uniform g is its declared local approximation with the error reported per region. Define the ledger per frame with explicit gravitational-potential, fictitious-force, frame-transfer and radiative terms, and make ledgers hierarchical so each closes at its own magnitude.
- **P04** [major, mechanism] Bend, yield and break are decided from joint and contact loads that the rigid tier does not determine physically, and quasi-static linear modal elasticity can neither vibrate nor buckle. Phase 2's gates (vibration frequency and buckling load within 3-5%, 'a bridge fails where the analysis says') cannot pass as designed for redundant structures, impacts or slender members; this is STRATA physics fatal flaw 2, inherited. — Evidence: DERIVED: at a 120 Hz contact clamp peak impact force is m*v*omega = 754 N per kg*m/s, while Hertz for a 1 kg steel sphere (R = 31 mm, E* = 110 GPa) at 1 m/s gives F = k*d^1.5 = 16.7 kN with k = (4/3)*E*sqrt(R) and d = (5*m*v^2/(4k))^0.4 = 75 um, a 22x gap. The STRATA physics review's numerical check (its DERIVED figures) gave tip-deflection errors of 23/13/7% for 4/8/16 free-free modes loaded through a weld, showed redundant load sharing follows soft-constraint compliance and sweep order, and that members with slenderness above 199 buckle below the 0.2-yield screen. — Fix: Solve each bonded or jointed assembly as an elastic network with real stiffness ratios (direct factorisation per island), bake Craig-Bampton attachment modes and geometric-stiffness buckling factors, and use impulse or energy criteria for impact damage. Make modes below the resolvable frequency dynamic state (DERIVED: a 1 m x 1 cm steel cantilever's first mode is 0.56*5048*0.00289/1^2 = 8.2 Hz), and gate with a redundant truss against the stiffness method through the runtime weld port.
- **P05** [major, matter] Two-way coupling between the bit-exact CPU tier and the f32 GPU tier is one sentence with no scheme. A lagged partitioned exchange is where momentum stops being 'exact by construction' and where floating bodies go unstable, so Phase 3's boat gate either fails or is passed by an undeclared stabiliser. — Evidence: DERIVED: a hull with 5 cm draft heaves at T = 2*pi*sqrt(0.05/9.81) = 0.45 s (omega = 14 rad/s); a lag tau on the restoring force gives negative damping omega*tau/2 = 12% at one frame and 23-35% at the 2-3 frame readback delay two reviews assumed (ESTIMATE, unmeasured on iOS). Three reviews cite the added-mass instability of loosely coupled incompressible schemes when added mass exceeds body mass (from memory, unverified); a floating body displaces its own mass, so every boat sits near that limit. — Fix: Put rigid-body degrees of freedom inside the pressure projection (or integrate coupled bodies' free motion on the GPU), use a fixed N-tick delay line, and record the quantised wrench stream as a replay input. Gate with a bobbing float at density ratios 0.1-0.5, heave period and decay, and a 60 s at-rest drift test; measure readback latency in Phase 0a.
- **P06** [major, matter] The 'one sanctioned departure' (rigid solids, incompressible liquids) does not cover sand, mud or soft solids, which the matter tier still promises. Explicit elasticity at 2-8 substeps forces their moduli down by orders of magnitude, the reviews' softening-admissibility rule was dropped rather than met, and materials between gel and rigid (rubber, foam) have no honest representation in either tier. — Evidence: DERIVED: c_max = dx*60*n at CFL 1, so at dx 2 cm and n = 2/4/8 the cap is 2.4/4.8/9.6 m/s, a modulus rho*c^2 of 5.8/23/92 kPa at 1000 kg/m^3. DERIVED: tyre rubber at 5 MPa and 1100 kg/m^3 (review's figures, ESTIMATE) has c = 67 m/s, 56 substeps per frame; 'a few litres of sand' (3 L, a 14.4 cm cube) strains g*h/c^2 = 6.1% at its base at 4 substeps and 2 cm cells, or 24.5% at 1 cm cells. Column collapse and angle of repose depend mainly on friction angle, so the Phase 4 gates would not detect it. — Fix: Treat the volumetric response of all dense matter with a unilateral implicit pressure projection and cap only the deviatoric modulus, or use an implicit or rigid-plastic granular solve. Add static-bed settlement and bearing-capacity gates at the shipped tier, restore the admissibility rule, and print 'unsupported' on the fidelity record for the gel-to-rigid gap until a representation exists.
- **P07** [major, scale] Liquid, ground and (absent) air exist only as awake particles or rigid bodies inside a fixed window; there is no analytic or field far-field. So the boat floats in litres, the digger has litres of soil, and a planet's ocean, terrain and atmosphere have no representation the window could be embedded in. — Evidence: DERIVED: 10-30 thousand particles (plan estimate) at 8 per 2 cm cell is 1 mL each, i.e. 10-30 L. The Hamilton physics fix (Tier 1 analytic hydrostatic reservoir with the particle window embedded) and STRATA's kept idea (integrated-pressure buoyancy on analytic and heightfield liquids) are both absent from the plan. — Fix: Add field representations for bulk media to Layer 1 (hydrostatic or heightfield liquid, terrain as heightfield or SDF with a material, ambient gas), with Archimedes buoyancy integrated over the wetted hull as a convergent-tier result. Embed the particle window through a two-way flux boundary with mass and wave-reflection gates, and let the window move and re-orient with the local frame and gravity.
- **P08** [major, mechanism] 'Contact stiffness and friction are derived from material data, then clamped' presents a clamp as a material law: above the clamp every solid has the same contact stiffness, so the derivation is vacuous. The Soft Step lineage also brings tuned constants and silent clamps the plan does not list among its declared approximations (charter rule 3), and 'the one sanctioned departure' is false by at least three (contact clamp, softened granular/soft matter, solver constants). — Evidence: DERIVED: at a 120 Hz clamp k = m*(2*pi*120)^2 = 5.7e5 N/m per kg for any material (STRATA physics review). MEASURED (box2d src/types.c and src/solver.c, fetched 2026-11-24): contactHertz 30, contactDampingRatio 10, contactSpeed (push-out) 3 m/s, restitutionThreshold 1 m/s, maximumLinearSpeed 400 m/s enforced by rescaling velocity, sleepThreshold 0.05 m/s, and a source comment that time-of-impact 'timeloss means there is a lost gravity contribution'. — Fix: State contact stiffness as a numerical regularisation, not a material property, and compute Hertz quantities (patch radius, peak-force estimates) analytically from the real moduli. List every solver constant, clamp and sleep rule in the Reality Report with a sensitivity test, and book any momentum or energy they remove.
- **P09** [major, verification] The ledger's 'numerical loss' account is undefined; if it is the residual, closure is automatic and the audit proves nothing. In a soft-constraint solver most contact energy leaves through numerical damping, so the physical/numerical split is undefined exactly where most energy is dissipated. — Evidence: DERIVED: an implicit soft constraint keeps 1/(1+(h*omega)^2) of modal energy per substep, 29% at h*omega = pi/2. Two reviews (Hamilton physics, Materia physics) asked for independently computed channels and a cap; the plan kept only the phrase 'a separate numerical loss account'. — Fix: Compute every physical channel independently (friction work, restitution loss defined as (1-e^2) of normal relative kinetic energy, viscous and plastic dissipation, Joule heat) and define numerical loss as what remains. Gate numerical loss as a maximum fraction of physical dissipation on named scenes (ring-down, rebound height, slosh decay).
- **P10** [major, mechanism] Stored elastic energy has no faithful home. In quasi-static modal elasticity strain energy is not a state variable (deflection is either cosmetic or moves geometry with no energy account), and springs integrated as implicit soft constraints lose energy at first order in the step, so bows, catapults, suspensions and springboards are wrong by tens of percent and depend on the step rate. — Evidence: DERIVED: energy kept over a quarter period is (1+(omega*h)^2)^(-pi/(2*omega*h)), about exp(-(pi/2)*omega*h), so at 480 steps per second a 5/10/20 Hz spring delivers about 90/81/66% of its stored energy. The Hamilton physics review's numeric check for a backward-Euler update gave 90/79/60%. — Fix: Integrate resolvable conservative elements (springs, modes below the clamp) with a second-order energy-consistent rule and carry their strain energy in the ledger as state. Add spring-launch efficiency tests at 5-50 Hz and a ring-down test with zero physical damping.
- **P11** [major, mechanism] There is no tunnelling policy: no continuous collision detection, no stated speed envelope and no test. — Evidence: DERIVED: at 480 steps per second a body moves v/480 per substep, 10.4 cm at 50 m/s, so any dynamic pair thinner than that can pass through each other; at an orbital relative speed of 7.67 km/s it is 16 m. The research notes dynamic-versus-dynamic CCD is off by default in Box2D v3 and Box3D. — Fix: Always-on speculative plus time-of-impact CCD against statics, opt-in dynamic CCD, a declared envelope (minimum thickness times step rate) on the fidelity record, bullet and grazing tests, and frame-relative velocities so bulk orbital speed never reaches the contact solver. Declare hypervelocity impact out of scope.
- **P12** [major, matter] The matter tier's boundary physics is unspecified: grid-based colliders cannot separate matter across walls thinner than about two cells, separate matter bodies fuse through the shared velocity field, and 'coupled with friction' is not a physical boundary condition for a viscous liquid. Viscosity itself is unresolved for thin liquids, which the plan does not declare. — Evidence: DERIVED: two cells at dx 2 cm is a 4 cm minimum wall, thicker than any cup, pipe or model hull (two-cell limit from the Materia physics review). DERIVED: the cell Reynolds number U*dx/nu = 1*0.02/1e-6 = 2e4 for water at 1 m/s, so molecular viscosity is far below grid-scale numerical dissipation and water and light oil behave identically. — Fix: Use a per-particle side-of-surface test (CPIC style) or particle-level SDF projection, per-owner two-field nodal contact, and no-slip or Navier-slip for liquids with Coulomb only for granular matter. Print minimum wall thickness and the viscosity floor on the fidelity record, and add a zero-leak thin-container test.
- **P13** [major, foundations] Phase 4 adds conduction and melting with no heat sink: no ambient reservoir, convection or radiation, and no temperature state for rigid bodies. Fire, combustion and a gas phase are neither scheduled nor listed as out of scope. — Evidence: DERIVED: 20 W of motor loss into a 0.5 kg steel housing (c = 490 J/kg/K, ESTIMATE from memory) heats it 20/(0.5*490) = 0.082 K/s, +98 K over a 20-minute session with nowhere to go. DERIVED: a 300 K black surface radiates sigma*T^4 = 5.67e-8*300^4 = 459 W/m^2, the only sink in vacuum; the Materia physics review derived that the Stefan gate can pass while in-game melting is grid-controlled (boundary layer sqrt(alpha*t) = 2.9 mm after 60 s against 2 cm cells). — Fix: Give each rigid body a lumped or coarse enthalpy, add an ambient reservoir with a declared film coefficient and grey-body radiation with measured emissivity, and book both in the ledger. State plainly whether fire is in or out.
- **P14** [major, foundations] There is no ambient medium: no drag, no buoyancy in air, no ambient pressure, no wind. This is an undeclared approximation (the out-of-scope list names turbulence, not air), it is visibly wrong at workshop scale, and at system scale the same vehicle must behave correctly in air, thin air and vacuum. — Evidence: DERIVED: drag 0.5*rho*v^2*Cd*A on a 6 cm ball at 20 m/s is 0.5*1.225*400*0.47*2.83e-3 = 0.33 N (Cd 0.47 for a sphere, ESTIMATE), 58% of the weight of a 57 g ball. DERIVED: sea-level pressure of 101 kPa (standard value) puts 10 kN on a 0.1 m^2 panel, which matters for any sealed or evacuated part. — Fix: Add an ambient-medium field to Layer 1 (density, pressure, temperature and velocity as functions of position, supplied by the outer tier; vacuum is a value, not the absence of the feature). Use a declared quasi-steady surface-element drag and lift closure in the empirical tier, air buoyancy, and ambient pressure as the matter tier's free-surface boundary condition.
- **P15** [major, foundations] The thermal governor contradicts its own rule: reducing awake matter freezes matter the laws say should move, so outcomes depend on device temperature. Governor actions are not listed as replay inputs, time dilation is never mentioned, the web has no thermal-state signal, and the default 'drop to 30 frames per second' makes the '60 Hz for 20 minutes' exits ambiguous. — Evidence: Plan lines 76-77 and 121-122 against the bit-exact replay claim at lines 87-89. Three reviews (Materia feasibility, Materia physics, STRATA physics) asked for refusal or shedding to be replaced by declared time dilation with worst-case certification at authoring time; one (Hamilton feasibility) asked for logged shedding, and the plan adopted the shedding without the logging. — Fix: Order the rungs: render scale, 30 Hz presentation with physics unchanged, declared time dilation, and only then logged matter reduction booked in the ledger. Record every governor decision in the replay stream, certify scenes on worst-case awake matter, and write exits as sim-time ratio plus dropped-frame fraction.
- **P16** [major, mechanism] Rolling contact is unaddressed although the first mechanism milestone is a rover: no wheel or cylinder primitive, no per-substep contact refresh, no rolling-resistance property, no tyre model and no rolling test. — Evidence: DERIVED: with contact anchors fixed for a frame (confirmed in Box2D source by the STRATA physics review) a wheel unloads within the frame once v^2/r exceeds g, i.e. above sqrt(9.81*0.05) = 0.70 m/s for a 5 cm wheel and 1.7 m/s for a 0.3 m wheel, giving 60 Hz traction ripple. Hertz patch radius scales as E^(-1/3), so a patch computed from a clamped modulus is 27-126x too large (Hamilton physics review, DERIVED there). — Fix: Add analytic wheel and cylinder contact refreshed every substep, a declared rolling-resistance closure computed from real moduli, and for vehicles a brush-tyre or enclosed-gas tyre model. Gate Phase 1 with rolling acceleration ((5/7)*g*sin(theta) for a sphere) and traction at speed.
- **P17** [major, verification] No gate is bound to the shipped configuration, so tests can pass on the f64 reference at fine resolution while the phone runs something never validated. Some stated tolerances are unreachable at the stated particle budget, and tests for the artifacts that break real mechanisms are absent. — Evidence: ESTIMATE: the free surface is located to about +/-0.5 cell; DERIVED from that: hydrostatic error is 0.5/N for an N-cell column, so 3% needs N >= 17, and a 17-cell cube at 8 particles per cell is 39 thousand particles, above the plan's 10-30 thousand. The STRATA physics review derived that a 5% waterline needs a hull 30-60 cells wide because grid contact acts 0.25-0.5 cell off the surface. — Fix: Run every gate on production precision at shipped dx, dt, particle count and iteration budget and publish those numbers beside the reference. Add step-rate invariance (240/480/960 per second), long-run liquid volume drift, resting jitter, four-bar closure drift, loaded transmission ratio, gyroscopic precession, spinning-assembly angular momentum and static-friction creep tests.
- **P18** [major, roadmap] The plan says automatic rigid-to-deformable switching was dropped, yet melting (Phase 4), breaking (Phase 2) and digging all move matter between a rigid body and particles or create new bodies at runtime. None of that transfer is specified, so mass, momentum and energy at those events are unaccounted and runtime re-baking is unbudgeted. — Evidence: Plan lines 61-63, 100 and 110 contradict each other. The reviews' fixes (surface-layer promotion with remainder mass properties and reserved slots; pre-fractured or lattice fragments that stay plain rigid; fracture energy per crack area at two resolutions) are absent, and strain energy released at fracture is not a state in a quasi-static model. — Fix: Specify one audited conversion primitive (rigid volume to particles and back) that preserves mass, momentum, angular momentum and enthalpy, with reserved slots and the remainder's mass properties updated. Pre-bake fracture patterns offline, keep fragments plain rigid, and book fracture energy as toughness times crack area.
- **P19** [major, scale] Activity management assumes gravity pins things to a ground frame and one clock runs everything at 480 steps per second forever. In free fall nothing comes to rest, coasting lasts hours to years, and 'never change the timestep' plus bit-exact replay leave no way to skip time. — Evidence: DERIVED: one year is 3.156e7 s x 480 = 1.5e10 steps; a signed 32-bit step counter overflows after 2^31/480 s = 51.8 days; f32 seconds cannot resolve one substep after 16,384 s (4.6 h), where spacing reaches 2^-9 s. MEASURED: the lineage's sleep test is a speed threshold, 0.05 m/s by default (box2d src/types.c), which presumes a rest frame. — Fix: Define a dormant representation: an assembly frozen into one aggregate rigid body (or point mass) on an analytic or coarse-step trajectory with its internal state stored, plus explicit wake conditions. Use an i64 tick clock with a declared multi-rate mapping, allow time acceleration only when every active region is dormant, and record rate changes in the replay stream.
- **P20** [major, api] The builder API is a list of nouns and becomes a usable kit only in Phase 6. Strict mode forbids spawning, teleporting and velocity setting, which a streamed large world needs constantly, and the plan does not say what language games are written in, at what rate controllers run, or how a game learns that something happened. — Evidence: Plan lines 70-74 and the Phase 6 row. Reviews asked for an event stream, a controller library with step-rate servo blocks, an authoring binding, implicit joint-housing collision exclusion, measured mass properties with provenance and an SDK slice from Phase 2; none appear. — Fix: Specify the API contract early: authoring binding, deterministic controller runtime and rate, events (contact, yield, break, sleep, hand-off), scene queries, snapshots and a stock controller library. Separate engine-internal streaming and hand-offs (audited, conserving) from game escape hatches, and end each phase with a small playable slice.
- **P21** [minor, mechanism] 'Motor tests within 1-2%' is unreachable for transients of small motors under first-order integration at 480 steps per second, and the electrical time constant is below one step. — Evidence: DERIVED: backward Euler turns a time constant tau into h/ln(1+h/tau), about tau + h/2, an error of h/(2*tau) = 5.2% at tau = 20 ms and 10% at 10 ms (typical small-motor mechanical time constants, ESTIMATE). ESTIMATE: small DC motors have L/R near 0.5 ms against a 2.08 ms step, so inductance must be dropped. — Fix: Integrate the linear motor-circuit-rotor subsystem with its exact exponential update, declare the zero-inductance approximation on the fidelity record, and name the motor tests (stall, no-load, step response, efficiency).
- **P22** [minor, verification] The determinism claims are broader than the design supports: a phone replay reproduces on the build server only for scenes that never touch GPU matter, the governor or timing-dependent input, and 'twice the bodies natively with identical hashes' conflicts with how the lineage obtains determinism. — Evidence: The research (lines 193 and 251, citing Rapier's documentation; not re-checked by me) says Rapier's enhanced-determinism feature cannot be combined with its SIMD feature. ESTIMATE (Hamilton feasibility review): two performance cores shared with rendering give about 1.3-1.6x, not 2x. — Fix: Define a replay as snapshot plus inputs plus governor decisions plus quantised GPU wrenches, and state the determinism scope in the fidelity ladder. Set the Phase 5 body-count gate from a measurement.
- **P23** [minor, roadmap] Several headline numbers are stated more firmly or more favourably than the research supports. — Evidence: DERIVED from the research's MEASURED Box3D and Box2D M2 benchmarks: '2D carries 6-7x more bodies' is the box-pyramid ratio 9.1 ms/1.36 ms = 6.7, but for jointed bodies it is 11.0/3.28 = 3.4. The research's own scaling gives 15-28 thousand particles cool and 10-17 thousand after derate for a lighter native kernel (low confidence), and reviewers corrected to 3-8 thousand liquid particles and 1-3 thousand honest sand (ESTIMATES) against the plan's 10-30 thousand; '4.3 W sustained' is one unverified excerpt of a 30-minute run on an A16 phone that is not the iPhone 15. — Fix: Quote ranges with basis and confidence, give the jointed-body ratio beside the box ratio in the 2D/3D decision, and lower the pre-probe planning figure for matter to the reviewers' range.
- **P24** [minor, delivery] Delivery statements skip review findings: a new repository's Pages site under the same account shares the github.io origin, so 'keeps PulseVault untouched' is not true for client storage, and 'the whole loop runs from this Linux environment' omits that GPU kernels cannot be executed or Safari-compiled here. — Evidence: Hamilton and STRATA feasibility reviews: origin-wide OPFS, IndexedDB and quota; no GPU device or Vulkan driver in the container; WebKit's shader compiler not runnable on Linux. The plan also has no soak protocol, time boxes or kill criteria. — Fix: Namespace all storage or use a separate origin, add a software-WebGPU path in the container and a macOS-runner Safari compile smoke test, write the soak protocol, and give each phase a time box and descope trigger.
- **P25** [minor, foundations] Charter rule 2 says everything object-specific enters through geometry and material, but restitution, friction, rolling resistance and emissivity are properties of pairs, surfaces, geometry and speed, and restitution is missing from the material property list. Material data are also implicitly at room temperature and one atmosphere. — Evidence: Plan lines 19-21 list density, stiffness, yield strength, friction pairs, viscosity and thermal properties only, while Phase 0b's first demo distinguishes steel, oak and rubber by bounce. The STRATA feasibility review noted that measured data do not exist for most material pairs. — Fix: Add surface and pair records (static and kinetic friction, restitution as a function of speed, rolling resistance, emissivity) with source, uncertainty and validity range (temperature, pressure, speed), and label mixing-rule defaults as such on the fidelity record.

### Fixes from earlier reviews that the plan dropped
- [Hamilton physics, Materia physics] Compute the ledger's numerical-loss account independently of the residual and cap it as a fraction of physical dissipation per reference scene; the plan keeps only the phrase 'a separate numerical loss account'.
- [Hamilton physics] Torque-free spinning-assembly, spring-launch-efficiency, head-on-impact and Newton's-cradle conservation tests at the shipped step budget with sleeping disabled are absent ('Conservation tests pass' is unspecified).
- [Hamilton physics, Materia physics, STRATA physics] A step-rate invariance gate (different substep counts must agree on penetration, peak force, restitution and break outcome) is absent.
- [Hamilton physics] The requirement to declare every tuned solver constant with a sensitivity test was written for AVBD and was not carried over to Soft Step's contact frequency, damping ratio, push-out speed, slop and restitution threshold.
- [Hamilton physics] Stick-slip and friction-limit-under-transient-load tests, and refreshing the normal force inside the sweep, are absent.
- [Hamilton physics, Materia physics, STRATA physics] Dynamic CCD, a declared speed envelope and thin-plate bullet tests are absent.
- [Hamilton physics, STRATA physics] Loaded gear-train tests (ratio, backlash, efficiency over many revolutions) and loaded-joint separation limits are absent.
- [Hamilton physics, Materia physics] Deciding rigidity by structural mode frequency and giving members lumped compliance with a direct solve per bonded island, validated against truss member forces from the stiffness method, is absent.
- [STRATA physics] Craig-Bampton attachment modes with a residual-flexibility term, with cantilever and first-mode gates run through the runtime weld and contact port, are absent.
- [STRATA physics] Baked linear-buckling factors (geometric stiffness) and impulse- or energy-based impact damage criteria are absent although buckling load is a Phase 2 gate.
- [Hamilton physics] An enclosed-gas pressure-volume primitive, a brush-tyre closure for rigid wheels, co-rotating-frame tyre deformation and three-shaft (differential) gear constraints are absent.
- [STRATA physics] Cylinder and wheel primitives with analytic contact refreshed every substep, and rolling-acceleration and traction-at-speed gates, are absent.
- [Hamilton physics] Computing the contact-patch radius for rolling and torsional resistance from the real moduli instead of the clamp is absent (rolling resistance is not mentioned at all).

## Lens: feasibility

**Verdict.** The workshop plan is a sound innermost region but not yet a deliverable plan: its headline scales are the pre-review research ranges (my re-derivation for the sustained web build is roughly 100-300 awake boxes and 3-8k liquid particles, ESTIMATE), most phase exits either cannot fail or cannot pass on the phone, and about two dozen feasibility fixes from the six reviews were silently dropped. With the solar-system requirement the roadmap order itself must change: the layer declared permanent has no frames, gravity model or time-scale policy, and no phase ever shows a second scale. The combined scope is finishable only with a hard v1 cut: probe, then a CPU-only two-scale release (f64 point-mass system tier plus one adopted-solver rigid workshop in a local frame whose gravity comes from the outer tier), then mechanisms; liquids become v2 behind a measured go/no-go, and sand, melting, yield/fracture, the native app and planetary surface detail are deferred. From this container WASM builds and WGSL on software Vulkan are workable (llvmpipe enumerates), but Safari's shader compiler and the deployed site are unreachable, so a macOS-runner Safari job, a post-deploy CI smoke test and on-page diagnostics are prerequisites.

### Small-world assumptions
- One awake detail bubble: every body and particle budget is for a single region around one observer; two simultaneously awake regions (for example a base and a ship) would each get half.
- A single world origin with f32 state (the source design's +/-128 m limit) and an fp32-only GPU; no frame tree and no re-anchoring.
- Uniform constant gravity of fixed direction is implicit in every Phase 0b-4 gate (hydrostatic rho*g*h, dam break, angle of repose, waterline, block stacking).
- 1x real time everywhere: the fixed 480 Hz step and 'the governor never changes the timestep' assume no time acceleration (DERIVED: an Earth-Mars transfer in 5 minutes needs about 74,600x).
- 'Sleeping' means at rest in the world frame; on a rotating planet or in orbit a sleeping body is moving and needs rails propagation with ledger entries.
- One global ledger of mass, momentum and energy: DERIVED, Earth's orbital kinetic energy 0.5 x 5.97e24 kg x (2.978e4 m/s)^2 = 2.6e33 J has an f64 ulp of 2^59 = 5.8e17 J, so workshop-scale joules vanish in a shared account; the source design's integer-microgram mass ledger overflows 64 bits for any planet (5.97e33 micrograms versus 2^64 = 1.8e19).
- Snapshot equals the whole world and replay equals initial snapshot plus inputs from time zero; both assume the world fits in memory and sessions last about 20 minutes.
- Replay logs small enough to paste, with no stream of promotion, demotion, re-anchoring or GPU-wrench events.
- The 20-minute soak of one fixed scene as the phase exit; no test of transition spikes such as landing, where far-field matter is promoted into the detailed tier.
- The governor sheds 'awake matter' inside one scene; admission control across regions and worst-case promotion volume are not budgeted.
- A render load of one room-scale view; no terrain level of detail, atmosphere, far bodies or depth-range handling from metres to 1e11 m, all of which would draw on the same sustained GPU envelope as the matter tier.
- A material database of workshop solids and liquids (steel, oak, rubber, water, sand); no regolith, rock, ice, gases or vacuum behaviour.
- A charter law list without gravitation, and 'electromagnetic fields out of scope', which also excludes sunlight and radiative cooling, the only heat sink in vacuum.
- Validation oracles that are lab-scale closed forms and bench experiments (dam break, column collapse, cube toss, beam deflection).
- 'Memory is not the binding limit' holds because the world is at most about 20,000 sleeping bodies and one 64-cubed grid (source design's ESTIMATE); streamed planetary data changes that, and a GitHub Pages site is limited to 1 GB (MEASURED from GitHub docs) while a 30-arc-second global elevation grid is 43,200 x 21,600 x 2 B = 1.87 GB (DERIVED).
- Collision broadphase, islands and sleeping over one flat body list in one coordinate system.
- Strict mode forbids teleporting and setting velocities, but frame re-anchoring and rails-to-dynamic hand-offs are engine-internal versions of both and need a sanctioned, ledgered path.
- Phase 5's 'twice the awake bodies' and every 'done when' measure one scene at one site.
- Origin-shared, evictable browser storage is acceptable for a demo scene but not for a persistent multi-session world (Safari deletes script-written data after seven days without interaction unless persistence is granted, MEASURED in 01-research.md).
- The device-lab loop of one pasted result block per phase assumes one scenario per phase; a multi-scale engine needs a scenario matrix (surface, orbit, transit, hand-off).
- The player's finger as the only force-limited actuator assumes human-scale objects within reach at one zoom level.
- Web budgets assume a single thread shared by the mechanism solve, GPU dispatch encoding and rendering; any outer-tier work (propagation, streaming, generation) joins that same thread unless Workers are proven on the phone.
