Help

Claim a slot to reserve your place for 30 minutes, then upload your file. If the timer runs out, the slot is released automatically. Invite-only calls open after two recent scores of 4.5 or better, or five accepted files. Payouts go out by PayPal or bank after the buyer accepts.

Payouts

Every call is funded before it opens. When a file is accepted, the call's pay plus any mount bonus is released to you on the timeline shown on the call.

Safety

Never trespass, travel somewhere unsafe, or film people who haven't agreed. Keep faces out of frame unless the call has a signed release. Report anything unsafe through Contact.

Privacy

This demo stores your claims and scores only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Terms

Draft only: each call lists its license (for example, Commercial, non-exclusive, 1 year). You keep ownership of your footage, and the buyer gets the license stated on the call. Final terms need a lawyer's review before launch.
